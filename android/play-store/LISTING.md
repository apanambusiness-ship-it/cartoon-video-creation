## English
App name: APANAMai Studio

Short description:
Create editable posters, certificates, visiting cards and photo story videos.

Full description:
Create designs and photo stories with APANAMai Studio, a brand of ROY INDUSTRIES.

• Edit poster templates with your own text and photos.
• Create certificates and visiting cards from editable designs.
• Arrange photos into scenes, add captions and available voice/audio, and preview your story.
• Save projects and keep a complete project backup, including attached audio.
• Export supported image and video formats on compatible devices.

The current Android build opens the Studio website in your phone browser. An enabled compatible browser and internet connection are required. Voice, speech input and export availability depend on the device, browser and configured services. Some features require a valid membership. Paid services, when available, show their charges separately.

Support: apanambusiness@gmail.com
