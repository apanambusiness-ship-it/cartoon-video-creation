Original AI-generated artwork for APANAMai Studio. Built-in image-generation tool used.

Prompts: photorealistic portrait Indian greeting backgrounds with quiet central text space; roses/jasmine on paper, marigolds and brass diyas, lotus pond sunrise, ivory palace wedding arch, daisies/morning sky, Holi gulal, Navratri dandiya, Eid lanterns. Separate rose, lotus, marigold, jasmine, foliage, floral corner, brass diya and toran were requested as transparent photographic cutouts. No text, logo or watermark.

Project assets: creative-assets/*.webp. Original image dimensions and alpha are retained; WebP copies reduce download size. Collection contains 8 generated full scenes and 92 distinct compositions made from these original cutouts, not 100 independently generated photographs.


Elements expansion: 100 separate assets total: 28 realistic transparent photographic cutouts (including the previous 8) and 72 original native SVG graphics. New cutout subjects: sunflower, hibiscus, frangipani, orchid, red rose, white rose, lotus bud, tulip, lily, daisies, carnation, bougainvillea, banana leaf, palm frond, mango leaves, peacock feather, butterfly, conch, kalash and gift box.

Generation prompt: “Create ONE premium realistic photographic cutout element for a poster editor: [subject]. Entire object fully visible with generous padding, rich natural texture, clean finely detailed edges. Genuine transparent alpha background. No glow, no external drop shadow, no text, letters, logo or watermark. Beautiful studio photography, not vector or cartoon.” Each subject generated separately with transparent_background=true, then encoded as WebP without resizing or repainting.

Original vector files: creative-assets/*.svg. Categories: decorative frames, borders, ribbons, stickers, nature graphics, festival graphics and lines/arrows. Frames are decorative overlays; they do not automatically clip an uploaded photo. SVG thumbnails stay scalable; insertion rasterizes at 1024 pixels for existing safe project persistence and export.

Canva official documentation was consulted for category organization only. No Canva artwork was copied.


Year-round festival collection: 16 separately generated original portrait scenes, stored in festival-assets/*.webp at 1024 × 1536. Built-in ImageGen mode. Initial prompt: “ONE premium realistic Indian festival greeting poster, [festival and appropriate objects], distinctive composition, quiet greeting space, respectful religious imagery, no date/year/watermark.” A subsequent ImageGen edit removed headline, greeting, footer typography and logo holder, reconstructing calm background while preserving the scene. No third-party festival posters copied.

Final templates use these text-free scenes plus five native editable text layers (festival title, greeting, organization, person and contact) and a separate footer panel. Regenerate catalog entries with scripts/build-festival-template-pack.py. Selecting a festival embeds its WebP into the project for portable Save/export. Template dates are intentionally unspecified; these are reusable designs, not a verified annual festival calendar.
