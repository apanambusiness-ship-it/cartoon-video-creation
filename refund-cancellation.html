Refund, Cancellation & Service Delivery Policy

APANAMai Studio · Operated by ROY INDUSTRIES · Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy applies to digital membership and creative services offered through APANAMai Studio. The service does not ship physical goods.

1. Service delivery

Manual editing tools are available through the Studio website when the website and your browser are working. A membership or paid entitlement is activated only after the server verifies a successful payment. Please refresh the Membership page to check the status. If payment succeeds but access is not activated, contact us with the payment/order reference; do not send passwords, OTPs, or full card details.

AI poster, audio, video generation and prepaid AI credits are not available for purchase until the relevant provider integration, pricing and payment flow are enabled and clearly shown before purchase. A price estimate alone is not a purchase or a charge.

2. Cancellation of membership and recurring payments

A one-time manual ₹100 renewal does not create an automatic debit. Recurring payments may be collected only if the customer separately and explicitly authorises an available AutoPay mandate and the payment provider/bank approves it. The intended plan is ₹10 registration with an initial 15-day period, followed by ₹100 every 30 days only under an approved and active mandate; AutoPay availability is subject to provider activation and is not promised while it remains unavailable on the Membership page.

If an AutoPay mandate is active, cancel it using the cancellation controls shown in Membership/provider or bank UPI mandate settings before the next scheduled debit. If the cancellation control is unavailable or the status is unclear, email us promptly with your account email and mandate reference so we can guide you. Keep the provider/bank cancellation confirmation. Cancelling a mandate prevents future recurring debits when the provider confirms cancellation; it does not by itself reverse a payment already completed.

3. Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed individually, including duplicate charges, a successful debit with failed or missing activation, or a material technical failure that prevented the purchased digital service from being supplied. Please email apanambusiness@gmail.com with the registered account email, order/payment reference, date, amount and a short description. Do not include passwords, OTPs, full card numbers or other authentication secrets.

We will check the server membership record and payment provider status before deciding the request. Approved refunds are submitted through the payment provider to the original payment method where supported; the provider/bank controls the final processing time. We will communicate the outcome or request any information reasonably needed for verification. A refund is not guaranteed solely because a user changes their mind after using a digital service, but every request will be considered fairly and applicable consumer rights remain unaffected.

4. Failed, pending or duplicate payments

If a payment is pending or the bank shows a debit but the Studio status has not updated, first refresh the Membership page and allow the provider status to settle. Do not repeatedly retry while the first transaction is unresolved. Contact support with the order reference. Duplicate successful charges will be investigated against provider records.
